Tucson Sermon Integrity Map

Protestant churches in the Tucson area and whether their preaching has been reviewed for plagiarism or AI-written sermons.

What the colours mean

  • Flagged - evidence meeting the project rubric that a preaching pastor delivered another preacher's material without credit, or that sermons show converging signs of AI generation.
  • Cleared - every preaching pastor was reviewed against likely sources and no meaningful signal was found.
  • Partially reviewed - some pastors reviewed or a result was inconclusive (for example captions disabled).
  • In progress - a review is under way.
  • Not yet reviewed - listed, but nobody has looked.

A dashed ring means the map position is approximate.

How reviews work

Sermon transcripts are pulled from the church's public YouTube channel and compared word-for-word against suspected sources (Alistair Begg, Tim Keller, John Piper, and others). Exact runs of eight or more words are counted, Scripture quotations are set aside, and the longest passages are read in context. A second pass looks for stylometric signs of machine-written text, which is a weaker signal and is only flagged with corroboration.

Each finding carries a confidence level and a downloadable report so anyone can check the work. This is an evidence review, not a verdict. Corrections are welcome through the project repository.
