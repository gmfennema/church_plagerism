Tucson Sermon Review

Public evidence reviews for unattributed text reuse and AI-writing indicators.

How to read a finding

  • Evidence threshold met — the published evidence meets the project rubric. Read the excerpts and limitations before drawing a conclusion.
  • No concern found — the review met the required coverage and did not find a meaningful signal.
  • Inconclusive — something was reviewed, but the evidence is not sufficient either way.
  • Review underway — research is active but nothing has been published.
  • Not reviewed — the church is in the directory, but no finding is available.

Outcome and confidence are separate. “Inconclusive” does not mean “probably concerning.” A dashed map ring means the location is approximate.

How reviews work

Public sermon transcripts are compared word-for-word against suspected sources. Exact runs of eight or more words are counted, Scripture and liturgy are set aside, and the longest passages are read in context. A separate review looks for changes in writing style; AI-writing indicators are only published as concerning when a same-preacher baseline and corroborating evidence exist.

Each finding carries a confidence level and a downloadable report so anyone can check the work. This is an evidence review, not a verdict. Corrections are welcome through the project repository.
